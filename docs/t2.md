# Sliver on Intel T2 MacBook Pros

**Status: believed to work, not yet run on a T2.** Everything below is covered by headless tests,
but no one has run Sliver on a real T2 yet. If you have one, see [How to help](#how-to-help). It
takes about 15 minutes and can't leave you without a Touch Bar.

Models, drivers and Esc per family: `docs/hardware.md`, "Touch Bar models".

## What a T2 needs

- A kernel with the mainline Touch Bar drivers (Linux 6.15+, or a t2linux kernel): `appletbdrm`
  (the bar's display, over USB `05ac:8302`), `hid-appletb-bl` (its backlight), `hid-multitouch`
  (its digitizer, `Apple Inc. Touch Bar Display Touchpad`). `hid-appletb-kbd` is the firmware's
  own F-key mode, used only when no daemon owns the display.
- The Sliver RPM. Its udev rule (`99-z-sliverd.rules`) does what the bar needs without tiny-dfr:
  - switches the bar's USB device to configuration 2 (display mode). Configuration 1 is
    keyboard-only and has no display.
  - puts the bar's DRM card and digitizer on their own seat (`seat-touchbar`), so the compositor
    doesn't take them as a second screen and a touchscreen.
  - lets sliverd (unprivileged) write the bar backlight.
  - starts sliverd when the digitizer appears.

  With tiny-dfr also installed, these lines are no-ops: same values, and the USB switch only
  fires while the configuration is still 1. tiny-dfr stays the fallback if sliverd keeps failing.

## How a T2 differs from Apple Silicon

| | Apple Silicon (M1/M2) | Intel T2 |
|---|---|---|
| Display | `adp`, MIPI DSI, flushed with DIRTYFB | `appletbdrm`, each DIRTYFB becomes a USB transfer (flush cost unmeasured) |
| Bar length | 2008 px | 2170 px (2018–19, no physical Esc) or 2008 px (16" 2019, 13" 2020) |
| Esc | physical | on 2170 px bars **the safe layer puts Esc first** (and so does the default layout) |
| Backlight | 0–255 | **0–2**: off, dim, full |
| Touch | `apple_z2`, SPI | `hid-multitouch`, USB |
| Fn key | internal keyboard | internal keyboard (`Apple Internal Keyboard / Trackpad`) |

What the 3-step backlight means:
- `max-brightness` (0–255 in the config) maps to 1 or 2. Anything non-zero stays on: the bar is
  never turned off by a low setting.
- Dim is step 1, which may be barely dimmer than "full" on some panels.
- Adaptive brightness and a custom `curve` can only pick 1 or 2. They never pick 0 while the bar
  is on.
- Off (idle, lid closed) is step 0.

Rotation is read from the display mode (portrait modes are rotated), so it works whichever way
`appletbdrm` reports the panel.

## Covered headless (no T2 needed)

- **Discovery:** the `appletbdrm` card next to `i915`/`amdgpu`, and the digitizer chosen by exact
  name over sibling interfaces (`sliver-hw` `discover` tests).
- **Safe layer:** 2170 px with Esc first, on both layers, and Esc fires (`sliverd`
  `t2_bar_without_physical_esc`).
- **Backlight:** steps for active, dim and off, plus adaptive and curve on 0–2 (`sliverd`
  `t2_three_step_backlight_with_adaptive_and_curve`, `profile` tests, the agent's `display::scale`).

## Unknown until someone runs it

- Whether `appletbdrm` reports a portrait or a landscape mode, and its connector name.
- The digitizer's X range and direction: sliverd maps the evdev range onto the bar length, as on
  Apple Silicon.
- How long a full-bar flush takes over USB. Partial flushes should help more than on AS.
- Whether the hand-over between `hid-appletb-kbd`'s firmware mode and sliverd is seamless at boot
  and at resume.

## How to help

**Testers on Fedora: follow `docs/t2-testing.md`** (install the RPM, what to check, how to go back,
what to send). The steps below are the from-source harness path for other distributions and
for developers.

You need a T2 MacBook Pro with a working Touch Bar under tiny-dfr. Each step is bounded, and the
safe-swap harness always gives the bar back to tiny-dfr, at most a few seconds after the run.

1. **Read-only probe** (opens no device):
   `cargo run -p sliver-hw --bin sliver-hwprobe -- --sysfs-only`
2. **Install the harness** (one sudo step): `sudo harness/safeswap/install.sh`. See
   `docs/safe-swap.md`.
3. **Draw a test pattern for 5 s** and take a snapshot:
   `harness/safeswap/bar run target/debug/sliver-hwprobe --seconds 15 -- --draw 5` and, while it
   runs, `harness/safeswap/bar snap pattern.png`. Tell us whether the bar showed the pattern the
   right way round.
4. **Touch for 10 s:** `... -- --touch 10`. Slide a finger from the left end to the right end,
   then tap both ends.
5. **Real sliverd for 30 s:** `cargo build -p sliverd`, then
   `harness/safeswap/bar run target/debug/sliverd --seconds 30 -- --backend real -v`. Tap a few
   keys with a terminal focused, and hold Fn.

Then open an issue with the outputs of 1, 3, 4 and 5, plus `/run/sliver-safeswap/out/sliverd.log`,
the snapshot, your model (`cat /sys/class/dmi/id/product_name`) and kernel (`uname -r`).
